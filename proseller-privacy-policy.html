ProSeller

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2025

📋 Overview

ProSeller ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a Chrome browser extension designed to help eBay business sellers calculate exact profit, fees, and view competitor data on eBay listings.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information ProSeller accesses, how it is used, and what we do not collect.

🔒 ProSeller does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal user data. All processing happens locally on your device.

🔍 What Data We Access

ProSeller only accesses the following data from eBay listing pages, and only when you are actively using the extension:

  • Product title — to enable one-click Google and AliExpress search
  • Product sale price — to auto-fill the profit calculator
  • Page URL — to detect whether you are on an eBay UK or eBay USA listing

This data is read directly from the eBay page you are viewing. It is processed locally in your browser and is never sent to any server or third party.

💾 Local Storage

ProSeller uses Chrome's local storage to save your preferences on your own device. This includes:

  • Selected marketplace (eBay UK or eBay USA)
  • Seller performance level (Below Standard, Above Standard, Top Rated)
  • VAT registration status and HMRC flat rate percentage
  • Promoted listings rate preference

These preferences are stored locally on your device only. They are never transmitted to us or any third party.

🚫 What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect any personally identifiable information
  • We do not collect your eBay account details or login credentials
  • We do not collect browsing history or web history
  • We do not collect financial or payment information
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not use cookies
  • We do not sell, transfer, or share any data with third parties

🔑 Permissions Explained

  • activeTab — Required to read product title and price from the active eBay listing page you are viewing
  • storage — Required to save your calculator preferences locally on your device
  • scripting — Required to detect product information from eBay listing pages automatically
  • ebay.co.uk / ebay.com — Required to access eBay UK and eBay USA listing pages only

ProSeller does not use any remote code. All extension code is bundled within the extension package and runs locally on your device.

👶 Children's Privacy

ProSeller is intended for use by eBay business sellers and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
